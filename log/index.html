Strokes: free, fr, fs, back, bk, breast, br, fly, fl, butterfly, IM, IMO, prime, stroke, spec, NF, choice (counted as Fr).

Resistance / power: chutes, shoots, parachutes, drag socks, socks, towers, buckets, tethers, stretch cords.
Zones: REC, EN1, EN2, EN3, SP1, SP2, SP3; white (REC), pink (EN1), red (EN2), blue (EN2 on 100s and up, EN3 on 75s and down), purple (EN3, or SP1 on 75s and down), green (SP2); WU, warm-up, WD, CD, cool-down, easy, social (REC); aerobic, steady, smooth, drill, DPS (EN1); threshold, CSS, mile pace, think mile (EN2); VO2, 400 or 500 pace (EN3); 200 pace (SP1); 100 pace (SP2); fast, max, sprint, all out, AFAP, burst (set from the distance); bld, build, des, descend (marked Check).
Patterns: rounds (2x over indented lines, 3 x [note], 3x through ... by round, START SET / END SET), 100 Fr, 200 IM repeating to the set distance, 75kk/25sw, (25 kick, 75 swim), Odd = dr, Even = sw, fly/back, 6 strokes fast, first 15 fast, 9 x 50 - drill, kick, swim, 6 x 100 - 3 easy 3 fast. Rest lines like 1:00 and running totals like 1500 m 5:25 pm are skipped.
Rounds: 2x { }, 2 Rnds { } and 2 rounds repeat what's inside. When rounds differ, each round gets its own part: Rnd1 PB/Pads, Rnd2 Pads; Rnd1= dr, Rnd2= sw; [Rnd1 FR, Rnd2 Prime, Rnd3 Ch]; a line that starts with Rnd2= counts in round 2 only; By Rnd, Bk, 25Fr/25Bk, Fr; Des by Rnd [Pink/Red/Blue]. On a single swim, NegSplit [Pink/Red] is split evenly across the zones.
Notes: explanations in [ ] or ( ), and the workout's Notes section, are saved in the practice's Notes. A zone or color in [ ] still sets the zone, and ( ) with a set inside, like (25 kick, 75 swim), is still counted.
