3. Season Energy Zone Plan

Generate a plan to see this.
Rest defines the SP zones as much as yardage does. SP2 needs long, near-full recovery between reps to stay maximal; the same yards on short rest turn into SP1 (lactate tolerance). REC yardage is part of the plan, not filler: active recovery clears lactate faster than passive rest. Back-to-back fast days carry less neuromuscular risk in the water than on land, but the metabolic cost is real, so keep easier volume around the hardest sessions.
(Weekly zone splits are season-level targets, not a day-by-day schedule. How you sequence the fast sessions within the week is still the coach's call.)
